# Professionalism at Focus Bear Reflection

## Research & Learning
* **Professional vs. Unprofessional:** In a casual environment, professional means being accountable and meeting deadlines, even if we use emojis and casual language. Unprofessional is being late to meetings without notice or being disrespectful in chat.
* **Remote Communication:** Since we can't see body language, tone is important. I will use clear language, assume good intent from others, and respond to messages in a timely manner.
* **Feedback:** I will focus on the "work" rather than the "person." If I receive feedback, I will treat it as a learning opportunity rather than a personal attack.

## My Reflection
* **Clear & Respectful Communication:** I will double-check my Discord messages before sending to ensure they are helpful and inclusive.
* **Positive Environment:** I will help by being supportive in the `#general` channel and participating in team discussions.
* **Meeting Preparation:** Before meetings with supervisors, I will have my task list ready and a list of any "blockers" I am facing.
* **Proactivity:** Being proactive means looking for the next task in the milestone instead of waiting to be told what to do. 
* **Follow-ups & Escalation:** If I don't get a response, I'll send a polite follow-up after 24 hours. If it's urgent and I still get no response after another 24 hours, I will escalate to a team lead or the CEO.
* **What I won't do:** I will not ignore messages, I will not be disrespectful to teammates, and I will not "ghost" the team if I am stuck.
