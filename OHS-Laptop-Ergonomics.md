# Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) for Desk-Based Work

## Research & Learning

### 1. Risks of using a laptop without peripherals
Using a laptop on its own often causes "Text Neck" because the screen is too low, forcing the user to hunch forward. It also puts strain on the wrists because laptop keyboards are not positioned for a 90-degree elbow angle. Over time, this leads to chronic back pain, repetitive strain injuries (RSI), and eye fatigue.

### 2. Ergonomic Equipment
*   **Laptop Stand:** Raises the screen to eye level to prevent neck strain.
*   **External Keyboard & Mouse:** Allows the laptop to be raised while keeping the hands at a comfortable, flat position on the desk.
*   **Ergonomic Chair:** Provides lumbar support to maintain the natural curve of the spine.

### 3. Ideal Workspace Setup
*   **Monitor Height:** The top of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level.
*   **Chair Position:** Feet should be flat on the floor with knees at a 90-degree angle.
*   **Desk Setup:** Elbows should be at a 90-degree angle while typing, with shoulders relaxed.

### 4. Healthy Habits
*   **20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to reduce eye strain.
*   **Movement Breaks:** Stand up and stretch every hour to improve circulation.
