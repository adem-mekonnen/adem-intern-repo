# AI Tools for Development

## AI Tools Used
* **ChatGPT:** Used extensively during onboarding to understand company missions, competitive landscapes, and technical concepts like CI/CD.
* **GitHub Copilot:** (Planned) Will be installed in VS Code to assist with writing automated test scripts.

## Experience & Reflection

### What worked well?
* **Information Synthesis:** AI was excellent at taking raw research and formatting it into professional Markdown reports.
* **Explanation of Concepts:** It provided clear definitions for complex topics like "Code Smells" and "Guard Clauses" which helped me learn faster.
* **Speed:** It significantly reduced the time spent on documentation and setting up initial file structures.

### What didn’t work well?
* **Verification:** I had to be careful to double-check "strong claims" (like "Focus Bear is the only app that...") to ensure they were factually accurate.
* **Personal Touch:** For reflections like the company mission, AI can be too generic; I had to ensure I added my own personal thoughts and experiences.

### When is AI most useful for coding?
AI is most useful when you are starting a new task and need a "boilerplate" structure, or when you encounter a specific error message that you don't understand. It acts as a junior pair-programmer that can help look up syntax and best practices instantly.
