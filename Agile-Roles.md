# Roles in an Agile Team Reflection

## Research & Learning
* **Developers:** Responsible for writing code and building features.
* **QA (Quality Assurance):** Responsible for testing the code to find bugs and ensuring the product meets quality standards.
* **Product Manager (PM):** Defines the product vision and decides which features are most important to build next.
* **Agile Project Manager:** Facilitates the process, removes blockers, and helps the team stay on track.
* **Collaboration (Kanban vs Scrum):** In Scrum, roles are very fixed (Scrum Master, Product Owner). In Kanban, roles are often more flexible, and everyone works together to move tasks across the board as quickly as possible.

## My Reflection
* **My Role:** As a QA Intern, my role is to act as a "gatekeeper" for quality. I contribute by testing new releases and reporting issues before they reach the user.
* **Interactions:** I interact most with Developers. When they finish a feature, I test it. If I find a bug, I send it back to them with clear instructions on how to fix it.

## Task Summary
* **How I fit in:** I help the team maintain a high speed of delivery without sacrificing quality. By finding bugs early, I prevent bigger problems later.
* **Frequent Collaborator:** I collaborate most with **Developers**. 
* **Improving Teamwork:** I can improve teamwork by providing very detailed bug reports (including screenshots and logs) so that Developers can fix issues faster without having to ask me follow-up questions.
