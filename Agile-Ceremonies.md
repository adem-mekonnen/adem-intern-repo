# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration Reflection

## Research & Learning
* **Daily Stand-ups:** Short daily meetings to discuss: What did I do yesterday? What will I do today? Are there any blockers?
* **Continuous Prioritization (Kanban):** Instead of one big planning meeting, we constantly look at the backlog and pick the next most important task.
* **Retrospectives:** Meetings held to discuss what went well and what can be improved in our process.
* **Backlog Refinement:** Reviewing future tasks to make sure they have enough detail to be worked on.
* **Asynchronous Collaboration:** We use Discord and GitHub comments to communicate so people in different time zones can stay updated without needing a meeting.

## My Reflection
* **Alignment:** Ceremonies ensure everyone is working on the right priority and no one is stuck on a problem for too long.
* **Most Important Ceremony:** For a QA Intern, the **Daily Stand-up** is most important because it's where I hear which features are moving to "Ready for Testing."

## Task Summary
* **Stand-up Observation:** I observed/attended the stand-up. I noticed updates are quick and focused on progress and blockers.
* **Collaboration Improvement:** I will make sure to update my task status on the Kanban board immediately so developers know exactly when I have started or finished testing their work.
