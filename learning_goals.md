# Learning Goals - Adem Mekonnen

### 1. What do you hope to learn from this internship?
I hope to learn how a professional software team operates using Agile and Kanban. Specifically, I want to see the end-to-end process of how a feature is requested, developed, tested, and finally deployed to users.

### 2. What skills are you most excited to develop?
I am most excited to move from manual testing to automated testing. I want to learn how to write scripts (using frameworks like Playwright or Cypress) that can test the app automatically, saving time and improving reliability.

### 3. What do you think you can contribute to the team?
I can contribute a high level of attention to detail and a "user-first" perspective. As shown in my initial QA report, I am good at finding edge cases and logic errors that might be overlooked during development. I am also committed to providing clear, actionable bug reports.

### 4. What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?
I feel less confident in my knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and how automated tests are integrated into the build process. I would like to improve my technical understanding of how code moves from a local machine to the production server.

### 5. What does success in this internship look like for you?
Success for me looks like becoming a valued member of the team who helps ship high-quality code. By the end of the internship, I want to have successfully written and merged several automated test suites that actively protect the Focus Bear app from regressions.
