# Company Reflection – Focus Bear

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
I learned from the website that Focus Bear was founded by Jeremy Nagel, who was diagnosed with ADHD and Autism as an adult. He realized that most productivity apps are too easy to "cheat" on or ignore. He created Focus Bear to be a "strict but kind" digital coach that provides the structure neurodivergent brains often lack. It was designed to prevent the "infinite scroll" and help people actually start their day with momentum instead of jumping straight into stressful emails or social media.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Focus Bear is solving the problem of **Executive Dysfunction**. It is a major challenge for people with ADHD and Autism where it feels physically difficult to start a task or switch between activities. The app solves this by making the Morning Routine un-skippable and using "Smart Blocking." Unlike other blockers that just use a list of URLs, Focus Bear can actually analyze if a site is relevant to the user's current "Focus Block," reducing the "decision fatigue" of constantly updating blocklists.

## Why do you think this mission is important?
The company mission is important because neurodivergent individuals often have incredible talents but struggle with the "boring" administrative parts of life, like staying on schedule or taking breaks. Without a tool like Focus Bear, many of these people burn out or lose their jobs. By providing an "external brain" to handle the routines, Focus Bear allows them to focus on their creative work and live a more balanced, healthy life.

## How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?
As a software developer, I believe that technology should be an "equalizer." I am very interested in **inclusive design** and accessibility. Focus Bear aligns with my values because it doesn't try to "fix" the person; it changes the environment to suit the person's brain. I find it very rewarding to work on a product that has a direct, positive impact on a user's mental health and career success.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Yes, I definitely relate to the struggle of getting "stuck" on a distraction. During my time at university, I have often sat down to study my class but ended up spending an hour on social media like tiktok, youtube  without even realizing it. 

I also know my brother who has Autism/Focus issues. I have seen them "hyperfocus" on a project for so long that they forget to eat or even stand up, which leads to them being completely exhausted the next day. I can see how Focus Bear’s mandatory micro-breaks would have helped them maintain their energy throughout the day.
